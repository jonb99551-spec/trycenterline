Is there an app to place traffic signs, cones and barricades on a PDF site plan?

Yes. Centerline Takeoff & MOT is a Mac app that places traffic-control signs, cones, drums, barricades, arrow boards and pedestrian-control symbols directly on your PDF plans, then exports a marked-up PDF. Browser tools such as CPDG Engineering, Traffic Chart and TrafficCAD also place these devices, but they mostly draw on a map or a drawn roadway rather than on your own PDF sheet.

How it works in Centerline

  1. Open the PDF with File > Open Plan.
  2. Set the page scale or calibrate against a known distance, so symbols and measurements sit at scale.
  3. Open the MOT / TTC Library and place signs, cones, drums, barricades, arrow boards and pedestrian-control symbols.
  4. Add notes, arrows and hatched work areas to show the layout.
  5. Save an editable project, then export a marked-up or flattened PDF, or print.

Then review the layout against your project and agency requirements. Centerline does not certify compliance or provide an engineering seal.

Common follow-up questions

Can I do this in a general PDF editor? Partly. You can add stamps or shapes, but you would need to build your own symbol set and keep it at scale yourself.

Does Centerline count the devices I place? Centerline has count takeoffs for counting items on a plan.

Can I put my own PDF site plan on a map instead? EasySiteSI pins a site plan PDF on a live map with three reference points, mainly for site logistics. Centerline works on the PDF itself.

Do I need a Mac? For Centerline, yes: macOS 14 or later. It has no Windows or iPad version. The browser tools below run on most computers.
