When RapidPlan may fit better

If your team works on Windows or prefers a browser app, wants to draw plans on Google Maps or Mapbox, needs RapidPlan's drawing and map tools, or has existing RapidPlan templates and workflows, RapidPlan is a reasonable choice.

When Centerline may fit

You want a native Mac app, you start from PDF plan sheets, and you want takeoff quantities and MOT layouts in one app.
