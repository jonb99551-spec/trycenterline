What is an affordable PlanSwift alternative for Mac?

PlanSwift is Windows desktop software (Essential lists at $2,000/year), so affordable Mac alternatives are browser tools such as Solid Takeoff, TakeoffQuote, Easy Takeoffs and TakeCost, or native Mac apps. Centerline Takeoff & MOT is a native Mac option at $9.99/month or $79.99/year that does linear, area and count takeoffs from PDF plans, and it adds maintenance-of-traffic (MOT) drafting.

Before you switch

  • List what you use in PlanSwift. If you rely on assemblies, pricing and custom estimating, look for a tool that prices the work. Centerline measures quantities and exports them to CSV or Excel; it has no assemblies.
  • Decide on browser or native. Browser tools keep projects in the cloud and often work on iPad. Centerline keeps plans on your Mac and is Mac-only.
  • Count the hidden cost of a VM. Running PlanSwift in Parallels adds a Parallels subscription and a Windows license.
  • Test on a real plan set during a trial. Centerline has a 7-day free trial.

Common follow-up questions

Can I run PlanSwift on a Mac? Only inside Windows, using Parallels or another virtual machine. There is no native Mac version.

What does Centerline measure? Linear, area and count takeoffs, with per-page scale or two-point calibration, named items, layers and quantity totals. Multiple plans open in tabs.

Can I get my quantities into Excel? Yes. Centerline exports takeoff reports, CSV files and Excel workbooks.

Does Centerline import PlanSwift projects? No. Centerline opens PDF plans, not PlanSwift project files.

Is Centerline cheaper than PlanSwift? On list price, yes: $79.99/year versus $2,000/year for PlanSwift Essential. PlanSwift has deeper estimating features, so compare what you need, not just price.
