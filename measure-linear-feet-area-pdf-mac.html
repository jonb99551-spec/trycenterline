How do I measure linear feet and area from a PDF construction plan on a Mac?

Open the PDF in a Mac app with calibrated measuring, set the scale for that sheet (enter it or click two ends of a known dimension), check a second labeled dimension, then trace lines for linear feet and close shapes for area. macOS Preview can't do this because it has no calibrated measurement tool, so use an app such as PDF Expert, Adobe Acrobat Pro, or a takeoff app like Centerline Takeoff & MOT.

Step by step

  1. Open the issued PDF, not a screenshot or a resized print.
  2. Find the right sheet and view. One sheet can hold views at different scales.
  3. Set the scale. Type the drawing scale, or calibrate: click both ends of a dimension you know and enter its real length. Two-point calibration is safer if the PDF may have been resized.
  4. Check a second dimension elsewhere on the sheet. If it doesn't match, recheck the scale.
  5. Measure linear feet by clicking along the line. Use a polyline for runs with bends.
  6. Measure area by clicking each corner and closing the shape.
  7. Recalibrate when you move to a detail or a sheet at a different scale.
  8. Record the result with the sheet, so others can trace where the number came from.

Common follow-up questions

Can Preview measure a PDF? No. Preview can view and annotate PDFs, but it has no calibrated measurement tool.

Why are my measurements off? Usually the sheet was not calibrated, was calibrated to the wrong view, or was resized when exported or printed.

Is PDF Expert's measuring free? No. PDF Expert says its construction tools are part of the paid subscription.

How is a takeoff app different from a PDF editor? A takeoff app keeps a running quantity list. In Centerline, each measurement belongs to a named item (Linear, Area or Count) and a layer, with totals you can export to CSV or Excel.

How do I do this in Centerline? Open a PDF with File > Open Plan, set the page scale or calibrate against a known distance, name a takeoff item, choose Linear, Area or Count, and measure. Save the project and export quantities or a marked-up PDF. Centerline needs macOS 14 or later.
