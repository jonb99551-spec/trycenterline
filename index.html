Common questions

What are the system requirements?

macOS 14.0 or later. Available on the Mac App Store.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Use Centerline > Subscription to restore purchases or manage your plan. Restore using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles billing and cancellation; you can also manage or cancel through your Apple Account settings.

How do I keep my work editable?

Save a Centerline project as well as any exported PDFs or reports. Canceling a subscription does not delete your local projects.

Are AI and cloud services included?

AI suggestions require your own Perplexity API credential and may incur separate charges. Cloud publishing requires a compatible server; hosted cloud service is separate.

Does Centerline certify my MOT layout?

Centerline is a drafting and measurement tool. Verify scales and quantities before use. MOT layouts and symbols require review against the applicable project and agency requirements; Centerline does not certify compliance or provide an engineering seal.
