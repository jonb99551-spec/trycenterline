FAQ

Does Centerline run on Mac? Yes. It is a Mac app on the Mac App Store and requires macOS 14 or newer.

What does MOT mean here? Maintenance of traffic: temporary traffic control around a work zone.

Will my plan meet agency standards? Centerline is a drafting and measurement tool. Layouts and symbols need review against your project and agency requirements; Centerline does not certify compliance or provide an engineering seal.

Can I export to PDF? Yes, marked-up and flattened PDFs.
