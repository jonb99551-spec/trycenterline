What is the cheapest traffic control plan software?

Several traffic control plan tools cost little or nothing to start: Conemap and Traffic Chart have free tiers, DTCD Plan Studio comes with a $9.99 per user/month seat, and Centerline Takeoff & MOT is $9.99/month or $79.99/year on the Mac. The right choice depends on whether you draw on a map or on your own PDF plans, how many plans you make, and whether you need a Mac app, a browser tool or Windows.

What "cheap" depends on

  • How many plans a month. Per-plan pricing suits one or two plans. A subscription suits regular work.
  • Base drawing. Most low-cost tools draw on a live map. Centerline draws on the PDF plan sheets you already have.
  • Platform. Most are browser tools. RapidPlan desktop is Windows. Centerline is a native Mac app.
  • Extras you'd pay for elsewhere. Centerline also does linear, area and count takeoffs in the same app.
  • Review costs. None of these tools replaces an engineer's seal where an agency requires one.

Common follow-up questions

Is there free traffic control plan software? Yes, with limits. Conemap's free plan allows one watermarked export. Traffic Chart's first traffic plan is free.

What does Centerline cost? Free download, 7-day free trial, then $9.99/month or $79.99/year through the Mac App Store. Both plans include the same tools. Apple shows the final price at purchase.

Is there a per-plan fee in Centerline? No per-plan fee is listed. The subscription covers the editor.

Does a cheap tool make a compliant plan? No tool does that on its own. Conemap and Centerline both say their output is a drafted drawing, not an engineered or sealed plan.

Does Centerline use maps or aerial imagery? No. Centerline drafts on your PDF plans, not on a map or aerial base layer.

Are AI features extra in Centerline? Optional AI suggestions need your own Perplexity API key, with separate charges.

Summary
